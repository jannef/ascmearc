# Procedural generation and special rooms

Developer notes for working on this part of Ascmearc (moved from CLAUDE.md, #185); the
rules it implements are in spec/07-generation.md; special
rooms are in `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`. Keep it current with the code, as CLAUDE.md says.

Procedural generation lives in `ascmearc-core`: `rng.rs` (ChaCha8 streams derived
from the run seed: `map/<level id>` per generated level; in play every rule draws by a key of its
own, `<rule>/<decision>/<actor key>@<second>#<n>` (`rng::draw!`, "Keys" in `rng.rs`: no shared
keyed stream, #1662);
`parse_seed` for `--seed`; `Rng::weighted` the one weighted draw, a single `below(total)` and a
walk, `None` with no draw when nothing weighs anything or the sum overflows a `u32`, which the
loaders of drawn tables refuse with `rng::weight_sum`; `magic::loot::pick_weighted` is it over
`(item, weight)` pairs), `content/dungeon.rs` (branches in the `dungeons` table, each campaign's `data/dungeons/`: the Depths in
`campaigns/showcase/data/dungeons/showcase.toml`; level ids
`depths:N`), `content/vault.rs` (vault rules; authors write to `assets/vaults/README.toml`, which the loader skips, and docs/design/generic-vaults.md §7), and `mapgen/` (`rooms.rs`, `caves.rs`, `tunnels/` (below), `mines.rs`, `minster.rs`, `vault.rs`
stamping, `connect.rs` flood fill / corridor digging / validation). A branch level is generated by
`World::travel` on its first visit and then persists in `LevelState` like a hand-made level (level
states carry their own ambient and placed lights). Generation is integer-only; a level that fails
validation is regenerated from the next derived stream. A valid branch level then hides its
branch's traps (`mapgen::hide_traps`, from the stream `traps/<level id>`, so the map is the same
with or without them), then a few of its doors (`mapgen::hide_secrets`, secret doors, from the
stream `secrets/<level id>`, only where `connect::validate` still reaches the stairs and every key
without them; `docs/dev/searching.md`). On about one `caves` floor in six a pool has a deep middle (`mapgen/deep.rs`, #1020: stream `deep_water/<level id>`, `Rng::deep_water`, drawn once the cave is built and before `build_walls`, so a floor without it is exactly its old self; a blob of 40 to 70 free floor cells becomes water, those with all eight neighbours in it `deep_water`; kept off creature routes and special rooms, and undone if it would cut any walkable cell off). Rooms floors, vaults, special rooms and hand-made levels never have it. Last of all, after `animated::arm`, a branch floor may get **ore veins** (`mapgen/vein.rs`, #1390; tests `mapgen/vein_tests.rs`): stream `veins/<level id>` (`Rng::veins`), so every other step draws as before; draws: `[veins] chance` of `data/mining.toml`, the count, then per vein its ore (`MiningRules::vein_table` at `Content::floor_danger(id, visit.player_level)` with the branch's `ores`, `pick_weighted`), a start among the `pal.wall` cells, its size, and its growth (a random cell of it, a random cardinal) over `wall` and `void` cells alike, never a stamped cell, the edge, a secret door's cell, or a special room's box grown by one. Only rock changes (`veins_change_only_rock`); veins are the same with secret doors or without (a secret door is no rock either way). The vein terrains are made at load (`content/mining.rs::add_vein_terrain`, `docs/dev/digging.md`); `ascii` draws them `V`. The insta snapshots in
`src/mapgen/snapshots/` and `src/mapgen/local/snapshots/` flag any change in the generator's
layout: review the maps in the diff, then accept them deliberately. They hold the terrain and
stairs (with locks, secret doors, veins, special rooms, shops, and named places; a local area its
start and flow) and leave out what is put on it, creatures, objects, traps, and lights, which
churn with every loot or roster change (#1526); `describe` checks those instead
(`nothing_on_a_wall_in`: on walkable ground, one creature to a cell, chests and traps where they
may be). Loot (`mapgen::place_loot`: floor
items by the branch's `loot`, vault `loot` cells, each a grade or a pool of item classes,
`mapgen/loot_cell.rs::roll` at the floor's item level, `docs/dev/magic-items.md`) is rolled once a
map is valid, from its own stream `loot/<level id>`, so adding loot never changes the terrain of a
generated level. It does shift what follows on that stream, and `hide_traps` avoids a vault's
`[[pile]]` but not a `loot` cell, so turning a pile into a loot cell moves the floor's items and
may move a hidden trap (a trap may lie under a loot cell). A vault's `monsters` cells
(`content/monster_pool.rs`, `VaultMonsters`; `Canvas::monsters`) are drawn with the population,
just before it, from their own stream `vault_monsters/<level id>`, through the same
`danger::draw`; the population keeps off every marked cell, so a floor with such a vault moves its
population (not its map, traps, secrets, or loot), and only that floor. A hidden trap may take a
marked cell, which then stays empty (`mapgen/vault_monsters_tests.rs`), but never a cell of a
`route` the vault's map writes for the pool, walked from any marked cell (`mapgen::patrolled`, the
cells `hide_traps` keeps off: the draws come after the traps and walk a written route as written,
#2070).
A hand-made level with no danger draws nothing (every branch floor has one, #2067). On a
branch with a danger the initial population follows (`mapgen/population.rs`, stream
`population/<level id>`; `docs/dev/creatures.md`: its own folk, now and then a stray from every
hostile type of the campaign, populations or none, unless the branch's `stray_pool` says
`"populations"`), and, from the same stream after its draws, the **leavings** about every creature
it and the vaults' pools placed whose type has `leavings` (`mapgen/leavings.rs`: each kind's
count, `Leaving::count`, on plain floor within 4 cells that nothing claims, no creature, walk,
object, trap, light, lock, chest, shop, or kept-clear room, and off what vaults stamped but
for torn cloth, so a vault's map stays as drawn; a statue only with open ground all
eight ways round, `LevelTemplate::statues` holding a nobody of the branch's population, not
unique or dread, holding nothing (named, as the level is made, from the run's petrified dead in
the graveyard: `world/fixtures/graveyard.rs::name_statues`, one name a statue, the most recent first,
never twice on a floor, which statue drawn from stream `statue-names/<level id>`; decoration,
out of the state hash, `docs/dev/saves.md` "The graveyard"); webs and acid pits change the terrain, torn cloth joins the
pile), then what its vaults' empty chests hold (`chest::fill_vaults`, stream
`vault_chests/<level id>`), then the floor's chests (`mapgen/chest.rs`, stream `chests/<level id>`, one
floor in five by default; `docs/dev/chests.md`), then its treasure rooms' chests, gold, and
gems (`mapgen/treasury.rs`, stream `treasure/<level id>`; `docs/dev/special-rooms.md`; a
greater treasure room is a box with no way in but its breach, which `connect::validate` lets go
unreached), then every creature's gear (`gear::arm`,
stream `gear/<level id>`). Each step after the first lays things only where nothing earlier
lies, stands, or is trapped, and nothing it lays turns a cell with something on it into
terrain that cannot hold it (a chest, a feature, a wall): `mapgen/tests.rs`'s
`nothing_on_a_wall` (objects and creatures on walkable terrain, one creature a cell, none in a
room creatures shun; chests on chest terrain with nothing on them; traps and locks on ground, a
door, or a chest), checked by `every_depth_is_valid_and_deterministic` and its own proptest
`nothing_lies_or_stands_on_a_wall` (#661). Outside generation, a rolled quest draws its target from the keyed
stream `quest/<quest id>` (`Rng::quest`), once, as it is given, never kept (#422,
`docs/dev/quests.md`).

Named vaults (#339): a `[[dungeon.levels]]` range's `vaults` (`DungeonDef::vaults(depth)`,
checked and marked `Vault::pinned` by `Content::pin_vaults` once the branch's campaign is known:
a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign) reaches the generators as
`Needs::vaults`. Both stamp them first, each on a random spot among every one where it fits
(`vault::spots`; caves also need its tunnels), and fail the attempt if one fits nowhere, so the
next attempt's stream tries again; then the random vault as before. Caves first make room when no
rock is left big enough (#582: `caves::make_room` fills the spot, vault and ring, that takes the
least of the cave with rock, a random one of those, then keeps the largest cave again); without it
Fyrdelf 12, Wulfeshol 4, and Wyrtholm 4 failed all 16 attempts for a few seeds in a hundred.
`mapgen/seeds_tests.rs` generates every branch's bottom floor for 60 seeds and every floor for
20, and requires each to succeed; the long sweeps (bottom floors to 300 seeds, every floor to
100) are `#[ignore]`d and run weekly by the `Ignored tests` workflow (#1514, #1537). `vault::shuffled` leaves
pinned vaults out, so a branch without named vaults draws exactly what it did (no extra draws;
the Depths' snapshots are unchanged). Fyrdelf names the foreman's cabin on 1 to 4 and the
drake's hoard on 12 (`mapgen/fyrdelf_tests.rs`, with the branch's first-level snapshot;
all twelve floors are `mines`, below);
Fennmynster names the sunken crypt on its bottom, 8 (`mapgen/fennmynster_tests.rs`, which also
checks the branch's raised room odds and its lack of shops). The four lairs of the wanted (#426:
Wyrtholm and Wulfeshol caves 100×40, Bānfeld and Brocenburg rooms 80×21, four floors each)
name their criminal's vault on floor 4 (`mapgen/lairs_tests.rs`, with a snapshot of each bottom
floor and a check over four seeds that the criminal is there once and on no floor above).

**Branches and level plans** (#1834; `content/dungeon.rs`, the field reference
`data/dungeons.toml`): a `[[dungeon]]` table holds only the branch fields (id, name, arrival,
depth, kind with floors/clearing/top, below, bottom, `[dungeon.endless]`, `[dungeon.portal]`); its
`[dungeon.level]` table is the level template (`LevelSpec`, every field optional), and
`[[dungeon.levels]]` rows (`from`, `to`) give the same struct, layered field by field over the
template for their depths (`LevelSpec::over`; a table field layers field by field too, `danger`
is one value; a row is read by hand, `LevelRow`, so `from`/`to` sit beside the level fields and an
unknown field is still refused: serde's `flatten` would let it through). Each depth resolves to
one `LevelPlan` (`LevelSpec::resolve`): the generator with its parameters, the size,
temperature, ambient, patrols, traps, secret doors, hidden and lock ramps, ores, loot, chests,
key, danger and `Spawning`, room and shop odds, vaults, and hidden location, so any level field
may vary by depth; the generators, `generate_for`, `Content::spawning`, and the wiki read
`def.plan(depth)`. `DungeonDef::spawning` is the template's folk (what the branch's floors have
unless a row says otherwise), which the encounter pools, the quests, and the branch tables of the
wiki read; `Content::fill_generic` fills the template's and every plan's generic list. The checks
are on the resolved plan, not on which table gave a field: a depth with no `danger` (its item level, #2067)
(so no `dread_one_in` or `population` without one), a size its generator does not build (`GeneratorKind::size_limits`), a
`hidden` on more than one depth or on a portal or endless branch, `vaults` on an endless branch,
and a tower plan with `size`, `secret_doors`, `key`, `ores`, `temperature`, `vaults`, `hidden`,
or a generator of another kind. Endless branches and towers are branch rules that pick or size a
plan: `[dungeon.endless] caves_level` is a second overlay resolved to `DungeonDef::caves_plan`,
drawn per floor by `endless_plan`; `Content::fit_tower` sizes every plan of a tower to its
clearing. Saves are unaffected: plans are derived at load.

**Generator parameters** (`content/generator.rs`, `data/generators.toml`): a level's `generator`
is a table with its `kind` (`GeneratorSpec`, a serde internally tagged enum, `deny_unknown_fields`,
no `flatten`) and any of the kind's parameters, each optional (`RoomsOver`, `CavesOver`, ...,
made by the `params!` macro beside the full structs `RoomsParams`, `CavesParams`, `TunnelsParams`,
`PassageParams`, `TowerParams`, and the mines' and minster's `MinesSpec`/`MinsterSpec`).
`data/generators.toml` gives every kind's full table (`GeneratorDefaults`, every field required);
a dungeon file's table overrides just the fields it names; a row's generator of the template's
kind layers over the template's field by field (`GeneratorSpec::over`), one of another kind starts
from that kind's defaults. The resolved `Generator` enum (`Rooms(RoomsParams)`, ...) is on the
plan, and each builder takes its own (`rooms::build(.., p: &RoomsParams)`; the mines keep theirs
on the `Mine` under construction; the caves helpers the tunnels and passage share, `place_vaults`
and `light`, take `max_tunnel` and `lamp_block` as arguments, so those two are fields of the
tunnels' and passage's tables too). What moved out of the modules' constants: every tunable at
the top of `rooms.rs`, `caves.rs`, `tunnels/mod.rs` (the `Bore` struct included, now
`content::Bore`), `tunnels/passage.rs`, `mines.rs`, `minster.rs` (not the plan's dimensions:
`CLOISTER`, `CHAPEL_DEPTH`, `RANGE_DEPTH`, `CHANCEL`), and `tower.rs`. The mines' `openness` and
`lava` and the minster's `flood` and `ruin` are `ParamRamp`s (`MinesSpec::at`, `MinsterSpec::at`):
a number pins the value on every floor, `[first, last]` runs it on a line from the branch's first
floor of that kind to its last (`first + (last − first) × along / span`, rounded toward zero; a
lone floor gets `first`), and `{ from, to, curve = "square" }` by the square of the way down in
whole percent (`t = along × 100 / span`, then `from + (to − from) × (t² / 100) / 100`); the
two arithmetics are what the mines and the minster computed before (#1836, #1837), so the moved
defaults make every floor exactly as before (checked by `generator::tests` and the floors'
snapshots). `content/dungeon.rs` finds each depth's place among the branch's floors of its
generator's kind before resolving. Changing a default in `generators.toml` changes every branch
of that kind; the snapshots in `src/mapgen/snapshots/` and `cargo xtask gen-vast --check` catch it.

**Tunnels** (#1835; `mapgen/tunnels/mod.rs`, `GeneratorKind::Tunnels`): caverns joined by narrow
tunnels, between `rooms` and `caves`, for floors where open caves would give archers too long a
line of fire (Cealdhlāw 3 to 6, 84×34: tunnels leave more rock than caves, about 750 walkable
cells to the caves' 2 300 at 100×40 and the 1 500 of the first 120×48 size, so the branch's
population is its `least`, 25 a floor, where it gave 70). The map is cut into zones of about 14×9; in most a cavern is tried for, its
size drawn among chambers, caverns, and a few halls, its box kept two cells from the others, then
a small chamber anywhere per two zones to fill the gaps; inside the box one to four overlapping
ellipses of noise (rock thinning toward the middle) are smoothed with the 4-5 rule and the largest
piece kept (drawn again up to three times if too small, else its box is left free). A spanning tree over the caverns' middles (Prim's, nearest first) plus a few near pairs
off it (loops) are the links; each is a line from one cavern's rim to the other's, bent by midpoint
displacement, bored by a `Bore` (`tunnels::bore`): a width drawn every few cells, mostly 2, now and
then 3, now and then a 1-wide squeeze of two or three cells (a squeeze for certain after six
stretches without one; the second cell is a real chokepoint, 8-way too, on a straight run), the
wider stretches' sides now and then nibbled. One to three dead-end spurs end in a small pocket.
Then `caves.rs`'s steps (now `pub(super)`): keep the largest cave (at least 15 % of the inner area),
named vaults (`make_room` takes the floor share it keeps), 40 % a random vault (15 % a second),
pools, stairs, lamps and fungus, patrols, special chambers. No deep water (`deep::lay` stays caves
only). **Passage** (#1839; `mapgen/tunnels/passage.rs`, `GeneratorKind::Passage`) is its linear mode,
for the shortcuts (Ūtgang): waypoints from one side of the map to the other, never turning back
along the width, joined by right-angled hewn legs and bored steadier (longer stretches, hardly a
nibble, a squeeze after two stretches without one); a hewn hall (a box, corners now and then left)
at both ends and at about a third of the waypoints; three to six straight dead-end pockets, each
dug only through rock no other floor touches; the named vaults (where room is made for one,
`make_room`'s box of rock may cross the way and only its larger side is kept: an attempt that
has lost either end hall fails, #1961), then as many random ones as the
passage's `random_vaults` parameter draws (`[fewest, most]`, default none: `generator = { kind =
"passage", random_vaults = [2, 3] }`), only vaults with one entrance (two would open a second route; a vault a
passage names should have one too), each dug to from the nearest floor by a cut of its own, a
pocket ending at its door; an attempt whose floor rings round more than `pillar` (4) cells of
rock (a hall come too near another leg, a vault's cut grazing the way: a second route) fails,
a vault's own cells counted as outside, so what a vault holds is no ring; the stairs in the roomy
spots nearest the two ends (which end is up is drawn). No pools. The ring is measured once more
at the end, every cell not walkable counted as rock, so a special chamber's feature (a spring's
basin) set beside a pillar fails the attempt too. Both keep every tunable as a named
constant at the top of their file (#1834 moves them into data). Tests: `mapgen/tunnels_tests.rs`
(snapshots of both at a few seeds and sizes; proptests: every tunnels floor connected and
deterministic, a passage's stairs more than half the width apart and its floor ringing round no
more rock than a pillar, `holes`, and the Skeul Hir's too with its vaults, `way_holes`, the holes
of the way between the stairs once every piece hanging off one cell is taken away; chokepoints,
`cut_cells`, the cells whose loss parts the stairs, on most floors of both, loops round rock on
most tunnels floors, the Skeul Hir's waystation on every floor, and its stairs far apart at
the seed where room made for the waystation once cut the way, #1961).

**The Skeul Hir** (#1760, #1840; `campaigns/ascmearc/data/dungeons/skeul_hir.toml`), the deep
gnomes' old long stair: a branch of one passage floor, 120×30, between Gimmwīc (its `bottom`)
and Lytelscræf 2 (its `above`: `DungeonDef::above`, its first floor's `<` leads there however it
was entered, `generate_for`), both ends hidden until Peran's reveal (`docs/dev/overworld.md` "A
hidden way with two ends"). Its floor is called "Skeul Hir" (`DungeonDef::level_name`: a branch
of one floor goes by its name alone). Rewarding: `random_vaults = [2, 3]`, `loot = [6, 10]`, a
chest or two on every floor (`chests = { chance = 100, count = [1, 2] }`), and Powesva an Skeul
(`campaigns/ascmearc/vaults/powesva_an_skeul.toml`), the stair-cutters' waystation, named by its
one floor: a hall with Jacca's stone (a named place), two of the floor's pool, a bunk room, a
tool store (a pick-axe, a mattock), and behind a secret door their cache: a rod of digging, a
heart-crystal, four gems, a `rare` find, and a chest. Danger 14 (Dēopgang's first floor): the way
opens only to a player who has been to Gimmwīc; its folk are the deep's lurkers, the stone's own,
trolls, and the dead, `stray = 0`, 25 a thousand walkable cells.

Ways up (#1117): a branch's `below` (`DungeonDef::below`, checked in
`Content::validate`: a level of another bounded branch of its campaign) makes `leads_down` true on
its deepest level, whose `>` leads there (`DungeonDef::down_to`), and its first level's `<` is
`Content::way_into` whatever it was entered from, as an endless branch's is. The level below gets
one `<` per such branch (`Content::ways_up_from`), placed by `mapgen/way_up.rs` right after the
generator builds and before `deep::lay`: the roomy spot (3×3 free floor) nearest by walking to an
entrance of a vault the level names (`Canvas::named_doors`, recorded by `vault::stamp` for
`Vault::pinned` vaults), ties in row order, else nearest to its own `<`; no stream is drawn, so
every other level is unchanged and only Fyrdelf 12 changed (where its pools, traps, and folk fall). The link
is a plain `StairsLink` after the generator's two, so `World::travel` arrives on it from the
branch above. Tests: `mapgen/way_up_tests.rs` (Ūtgang's three passage floors and links, the top's
`<` however entered, Fyrdelf 12's second `<` within 36 steps of the drake for twelve seeds and
no other floor's, the `below` content checks).

A bottom (#1434): a branch's `bottom` (`DungeonDef::bottom`, exclusive with `below`, `endless`,
and `portal`) names a hand-made level of its campaign; `leads_down` is true on its deepest level,
whose `>` leads there (`down_to`). No stairs are placed: the hand-made level carries its own `<`
naming the deepest level (`Content::validate` refuses a bottom without one or of another
campaign), so `World::travel` arrives on it, and back up on the deepest level's `>`. Dēopgang's
bottom is Gimmwīc, the deep gnomes' town. Tests: `content/dungeon.rs` (parse and exclusions),
`content/validate.rs` (the validate checks), `world/campaigns/deep_gnome_tests.rs` (down to the town and back).

**Towers** (#1783, slice T1 of docs/design/wizard-tower.md §16; `content/tower.rs`,
`mapgen/tower.rs`): a branch of `kind = "tower"` (`DungeonDef::tower`, `Tower`) goes **up**
from a hand-made outdoor `clearing`, the ground floor, to a hand-made `top`, with `floors`
counting both (`depth` is `floors − 2`; `level_name` counts from the clearing, so depth 1 is
"Drȳtorr 2"). `Content::fit_tower` (in `load_dungeons`, the levels being loaded) gives every
floor the clearing's size and finds the footprint, the box of the clearing's tower wall; the
clearing's `<` to depth 1 must stand inside it, and the stair runs on from there: each floor's
`>` on the cell of the `<` below, its `<` in the interior corner farthest from that `>`
(`tower::far_corner`; `Tower::stairs`, known at load, so floors are made in any order), the last
cell the top's `>`, which must name the highest floor and stand there (the error gives the cell).
`DungeonDef::tower_ways` swaps the links `generate_for` writes: the `<` to depth + 1 (the highest
to `top`), the `>` to depth − 1 (the first to the clearing); both are on every floor, and
`World::travel` arrives as ever on the staircase that leads back. The `tower` generator (only a
tower's, and a tower takes only it) draws from the floor's map stream: the ring wall; the inside
divided into rooms and halls (#1823; `tower::divide`): a wall across the longer way of a stretch
(the other way where that has no room), with a doorway (a closed door 30%, an open one 20%, else a
gap; a wall of 10 or more cells a second one by half), then each side again; a stretch with a
side over 15 is always divided, one with both sides 9 or less never, between by 55%; a wall leaves
three cells of floor each side, never runs through a staircase, and never ends against a
doorway, so every room is reached; a window every third ring cell (never a corner, never an
inside wall's end), the stairs, and the light (wizards light their towers): a sconce in a corner
of every room, a second (the corner across) in one of 40 cells or more, one more (a long side's
middle) where a side is 14 or longer, none beside a doorway (a lamp if none is lit). On
Drȳtorr's 39×17 inside that is many rooms and halls (docs/dev/towers.md). `generate_for` builds a tower with its own palette
(`tower::palette`: the wall, the floor, and the outside in place of rock, so `hide_traps`, loot,
chests, and the population go on its floor), skips `build_walls`, and makes the floor
`LevelKind::Outdoor` (§7: sky, weather, and light come with the kind). No vaults, shops, special
rooms, patrols, secret doors, locks, or veins (`ores` is `[]`). The terrain is four ids in
`content/tower.rs` (`WALL`, `FLOOR`, `WINDOW`, `OUTSIDE`: `tower_wall`, `tower_floor`, `window`,
`air`; docs/dev/towers.md), and every floor gets `below = clearing` and `storey = depth`, so its
air shows the clearing (#1781). Tests:
`content/tower_tests.rs` (what is refused, the clearing's and the top's checks),
`mapgen/tower_tests.rs` (the showcase's test tower, `campaigns/showcase/data/dungeons/tower_climb.toml`,
scenario `tower_climb`: both floors' snapshots, the footprint and swapped stairs over twelve
seeds, the rooms every cell reached, a climb to the top and down again as a replay; Drȳtorr's
floors' rooms and a snapshot).

**Mines** (#1836; `mapgen/mines.rs`, tests `mapgen/mines_tests.rs`): a worked mine that opens up
and grows lava the deeper it lies. `MinesParams` (`content/generator.rs`, the `[mines]` table of
`data/generators.toml`) drives it, led by two numbers 0 to 100: `openness` (gallery width, chamber
size, how many and how big the natural hollows) and `lava` (how many lava cells: one per
`lava_share` walkable cells at 100; pools from a budget of `min_pool`, channels from `lava` 31).
Both are ramps over the branch's `mines` floors ("Generator parameters" below): `openness =
[0, 100]` evenly from its first mines floor to its last, `lava = { from = 0, to = 100, curve =
"square" }` by the square of the way down (Fyrdelf: 0, 0, 3, 7, 12, 20, 29, 39, 51, 65, 81, 100);
a row pins either (`generator = { kind = "mines", lava = 0 }`). Everything draws from the level's
map stream, in the order the module docs list: named and random vaults (40 %, then 15 %, as caves)
into untouched rock; one drive per 12 rows (`cheapest_path` between waypoints 10 to 20 cells apart,
bends dear, `turn`), cross-cuts between neighbouring drives, a tunnel from every vault entrance,
dead-end headings (rubble at the face, one in two); walled chambers beside the galleries, one way
in each (a doorway, or with `40 − 0.3 × openness` % a door); hollows (cellular automata, 44 %
rock, four passes, inside `openness × area / 80 000` ellipses), never into a chamber's box or a
vault; every cut-off part tunnelled to the first drive (under 8 cells, filled in); the stairs in
chambers' roomy spots (the `>` among the farthest 30 % by walking); lava, never on a worked cell
(gallery, tunnel, heading, chamber), a vault, an object, or within `lava_stairs` (2) of a stair,
its banks opened into floor, banks unreached filled again and a feature that cuts anything else
off undone; lava touching no walkable cell (nor lava that does) rock again; light (chamber
torches as rooms', a lamp on about every 12th gallery cell with one chance in two, fungus in the

kit is the shared `[keeper]`'s. Fixture branches in `mapgen/tests.rs` (`FIXTURE`) check that a
branch favouring only flooded rooms makes only flooded rooms and `shops = { chance = 0 }` no
shop.

Which vaults a level may get (#337): `generate_for` keeps those whose `Vault::stamps_on(def,
depth)` holds (a dungeon vault, shared or of the branch's campaign, on a branch or level its
`dungeons` names, if any) on the `Canvas` (`Canvas::vaults`, content order), and

`spaced_vaults` deals the branch's random vaults round the phases first, then keeps of this
floor's phase only those whose file's `danger` holds the floor's (`Vault::suits`, #1073): the
danger the pools draw at, `Content::spawning`'s rule at the `Visit`'s player level (0 on a floor
with none), read once in `generate_for`. On the endless branch that is the roster's top past it,

pools can draw. Asked after the deal, the field moves no other vault's phase, and draws nothing:

Which way a vault lies (#959): `vault::turned` draws one of the eight `content::Turn`s from the
generator's own map stream as each drawn vault is tried, then `Vault::turned` makes the vault a file
drawn that way would load as, and the generator measures and stamps that (`vault::spots`, `fits`,
`stamp`, and the rooms and caves code read its size and entrances, so nothing else knows). `ruins` in
`mapgen/local/` does the same. A vault that may not turn (`Vault::may_turn`: pinned, or the file's
`turns = false`) comes back borrowed with no draw, so a floor's named vaults draw what they did, and
`caves::make_room`, which only named vaults reach, works on the size in the file. A level that tries
no random vault draws nothing either; one that does moves after the draw, like any other change to
its stream. Rotation swaps width and height: a floor with little height may not fit a vault turned
across, which is then one more failed try, as for a vault too big. `content/vault_turn.rs` holds the
transform: `turned_template` destructures `LevelTemplate` by every field, so a new field with a
position or direction must be decided on there. It maps terrain, `creatures` (`pos`, `route`),
`lights`, `flow` (`dir`), `objects` (`[[pile]]`, `[[item]]`), `traps` (a bolt's `dir`), `locks`,
`chests`, `secrets`, `places`, `regions`, `loot`, `monsters` (cells kept in row order), `hidden`,
`ice`, `player_start`, `stairs`; `rooms` and `shops` are refused on a vault, and no terrain has a
direction of its own (a stair's `<`/`>` means up or down, a door or wall none). Entrances keep their
row order too. Tests: `content/vault_turn_tests.rs` (a quarter turn against the same vault drawn by
hand; every turn of every vault passes `Vault::new` again), `mapgen/vault_turn_tests.rs` (Flooded
cell and Guard post on real floors, pinned vaults, the draw), `ruins_are_outdoor_vaults_stamped_some_way_round`,

whose danger its `danger` holds (both branches run 6 to 9, so a vault of danger 8 and deeper

for, in 100 seeds, on Grundlēas's rooms floors and Fyrdelf's mines floors of dangers 9 to 16 (#1077), if

How often, and how rarely the same one (#960): rooms floors roll `VAULT_CHANCE` 20 %
(`rooms.rs`); cave floors 40 % for one (`caves.rs`), then 15 % for a second. Before it was 50 %
and 1 + 30 %: on the six shared vaults (three stamp in a dungeon) a 600-floor sample of the
Ascmearc's rooms and cave branches met 0.61 random vaults a floor, 184 of 369 a repeat within
its branch, Flooded cell 148 times; now 0.15 a floor, 9 repeats of 89 (the same sample: 20 seeds of
fyrdelf, cealdhlaw, fennmynster, and banfeld). `mapgen::spaced_vaults` (called by `generate_for`) keeps
repeats apart without any saved state: the branch's random (unpinned) vaults, sorted by id, are
shuffled by stream `vault_phases/<branch id>` of the run seed and dealt round `VAULT_PHASES` (4)
phases; floor `depth` offers only the vaults of phase `depth % 4`. So no vault comes within four
floors of itself in a branch, and the deal differs by run and branch. A level's own named
(pinned) vaults are untouched. Adding vaults raises the count a floor may choose from, not the
chance of one.
An endless branch (`DungeonDef::endless`, #1010; Grundlēas,
`campaigns/ascmearc/data/dungeons/grundleas.toml`) has one plan, read at every depth
(`DungeonDef::row`), and `depth` 1 for what loops over it; `leads_down` is always true.
`generate_for` draws the floor's plan from stream `floor_kind/<level id>` (`endless_plan`): with
`[dungeon.endless] caves` percent the caves plan (`DungeonDef::caves_plan`: the `caves_level`
fields over the template, so it may change the generator, the size, or anything the generator
reads; `Content::spawning` reads the template's plan either way), else the template's; and its first floor's `<` is `Content::way_into`, not `entered_from`. The whole
generation runs under the floor's own seed (`world/levels/endless.rs::floor_seed`) with the usual stream
labels, so `cargo dev-cli gen --level grundleas:3 --seed S` shows what that seed makes, not what the
run of seed S makes on its third floor (that depends on how many floors it has made). Spaced
vaults (`spaced_vaults`) deal anew for every floor under that seed: a random vault may come on two
floors in a row, which no one can compare. `Content::spawning` reads the danger at
`depth.min(roster_depth)`, filled by `Content::fill_rosters` from the highest level of the
branch's population and strays, so every draw (population, dens, hoards, spawns) stops rising at
the roster's top and a floor at any depth holds creatures; `Content::floor_danger` is the number
that keeps rising. The dread draws do not stop rising: `[dungeon.endless] dread` (`content/dread.rs`,
`DungeonDef::dread`) gives `dread_one_in` by the floor's rising danger, never its depth (#2094;
`DangerRule::rising`, what `Content::floor_danger` gives; `Content::draw_odds_at`, which
`generate_for` patches into the `Spawning` the population and dens draw by; docs/dev/creatures.md
"Dread"), Grundlēas's (whose danger is its floor number) falling from one in 40 at danger 70 to one in 5
from 100. Other caps past that top: `Content::item_level` clamps at `MAX_ITEM_LEVEL` as
ever; `hidden_at` and `locks_at` read the rising danger too, saturate, and keep rising (the stairs never hang on a lock or a
secret, `connect::validate`). Grundlēas gets its draws by `stray = 100` over the campaign's generic
list and names only the rat as a `population`, which every other branch's strays already hold, so
none of their lists move.

**The Pits** (#1296; docs/design/miscasts-review.md §7): a branch with
`[dungeon.portal]` (`DungeonDef::portal`, `Portal`; exclusive with `endless` and `below`, its id,
like every branch's, without `@`) exists only as instances. Hellegeat
(`data/dungeons/hellegeat.toml`) is the one, shared: `campaign` `None`, so it is in every
campaign's fingerprint and `has_level` finds its floors whatever the campaign; its population and
traps are shared, so the cross-campaign checks refuse nothing. A Pit's floors are
`hellegeat@S:N`: `parse_branch_level` gives `("hellegeat@S", N)`, `parse_instance` splits `S` (a
positive `u32`, no leading zeros, so a Pit has one id), and `Content::branch_level` accepts the
`@S` form for a portal branch only and only that form for it (`hellegeat:3` is no level), so
everything that reads `(def, depth)` (spawning, danger, item level, names, arrival, vaults by
`def.level_id(depth)`, `hellegeat:5`) works unasked. `generate_for` builds the neighbours from the
floor's own id (`content::sibling`: `up_to` past the first floor, and `DungeonDef::down_to(id,
depth)`), so a Pit's stairs stay in it; its first floor's `<` is `entered_from`, which
`World::visit` sets to the Pit's record's `from` (its mouth's level, `world/levels/pit.rs`, #1297) however
the floor is reached, a climb from the second included. Every stream is
labelled by the id, and `floor_seed` gives a Pit's floor the run seed (not `endless/<n>`), so a
floor is a pure function of the run seed, `S`, and the depth: `cargo dev-cli gen --level
hellegeat@7:3 --seed S` prints what a run of seed `S` makes of Pit 7's third floor (but for the
population, read at the player's level as the floor is made, and the first floor's `<`).
`Content::is_endless_floor` is true of a Pit's floor (`is_portal_floor` tells them apart), so
`World::forget_floors` forgets it when left and `EndlessFloors` keeps its `deepest` by
`hellegeat@S`, each Pit paying once; `EndlessFloors::made` counts only the endless branches'
floors. The loader refuses stairs and hidden locations naming a Pit's floor (`Content::validate`),
quests naming one or the branch (`quest.rs::names_level`, a kill quest's `level`, a pick's
`creatures`), and `below` naming one; `fill_generic` leaves a portal branch's population out of
every other area's lists, and `branches_of` (rolled quests) leaves the branch out. Tests:
`mapgen/hellegeat_tests.rs`, `content/hellegeat_tests.rs`, `world/campaigns/hellegeat_tests.rs`; the seeds
and deep-water tests generate Pit 1's floors for the branch.
