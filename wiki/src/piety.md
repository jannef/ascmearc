# Piety

Your god keeps an opinion of you. Deeds your [faith](character.md#choosing-a-faith) prizes raise
it, and deeds it hates lower it. Each dawn it moves a little back toward where it started. The
game never shows the number: the [character screen](character.md#seeing-who-you-are) shows a
word instead, your **standing**.

The opinion is a score from −100 000 to 100 000. Everything in the game today happens within a
few hundred points of 0. The rest of the scale is left empty on purpose.

Piety is not [alignment](alignment.md). Alignment is what you are, and the world reacts to it.
Piety is what your god thinks of you, and your god reacts to it. A Saintly ratfolk can still
displease the Never-Full.

## Your standing

Your standing is one of four words. Your [prayers](prayer.md) read it: a god that is glad of you
strains less at each prayer (see [Strain](prayer.md#strain)), and one that has gone silent does
not hear you at all.

When your standing changes, you feel it, in your faith's own words ("The Light is far from
you."). While it is not Heard, Pray in the [abilities menu](abilities.md#the-abilities-menu) ends
with the same words, so you know before you spend the turn.

The Frogs Beyond and the Big Ones keep no opinion. Their faithful are always Heard.

Every faith of the Light counts the same deeds, and every dark faith counts its own. Each faith
then adds deeds of its own, or counts a shared one differently. A deed's worth is added whole,
however high or low your score already is.

### Kills

A kill counts first as the kind of kill it is, from the tables above. Then each kind of creature
the victim was adds its own worth, and some gods count the state you were in. All of it is added
together, once. A plain kill of a hostile creature counts for nothing with most gods.

The Howl counts only kills made while [Raging](status-list.md#status-raging).

## Favour fades

Every god's opinion moves back toward where it rests, a little each dawn (at 06:00). Favour fades
if you do nothing to keep it, and anger fades too, in time. No god's favour and no god's anger
lasts for ever. Some gods are quicker or slower than others:

{{#include generated/piety-time.md}}

A priest's and a paladin's vows are a standing regard: their god starts a little ahead, and the
dawn brings them back there rather than to 0.

The scorn is where your alignment meets your god. The Light scorns the Evil and the Wicked, and
the dark powers scorn the Good and the Saintly. While your god scorns you, it cools toward you
each dawn instead of drifting back, until you mend your ways or it stops hearing you.

> **Example: a priest of the Mynster.** A human priest starts at 20, and the Light is glad of
> them from 100: 80 points away. Each consecration of an unholy place is worth 10 to the
> Mynster, so eight consecrations bring them there, before the dawns between take their share.

> **Example: a sister who turns Evil.** A warrior of the Mynster starts at 0. Once her alignment
> is Evil, each dawn takes 4. On the 13th dawn she is Unheard, and on the 50th she is at -200,
> the edge of Shunned, where the cooling stops.

## What piety does not touch

Piety never takes anything away from you, and nothing the story needs asks for it. It bars no
quest, no shop, and no way forward.

It does not touch the [ratfolk's craving](food.md#the-ratfolks-craving) either. The Never-Full
counts the meals the craving feeds on, but her opinion comes and goes with them, while the
craving never goes back.
