# Your pipe

A long-stemmed pipe is a [halfling](races.md#race-halfling)'s comfort on the road. Every halfling
starts with one in the pack. It is a [tool](objects.md#class-tools): it weighs 60 g and goes in the
**[Tool](equipment.md#what-fits-where)** slot of your [equipment](equipment.md), where your
[lantern](lantern.md) or a [pick-axe](digging.md#digging) would go, so you hold one or the other.

## Smoking it

With the pipe ready in the Tool slot, press `u` (use a tool) to smoke it.

- **Only halflings smoke.** Anyone else just coughs, and no time passes.
- **It wants a full belly.** You must be **Bloated** (see [How hungry you
  are](hunger.md#how-hungry-you-are)). If you are not, you are told "A pipe wants a full belly
  first.", and no time passes.
- **It takes an hour.** You sit back and [rest](resting.md#how-resting-works) for one hour. You
  heal as you would resting, and the smoke stops for the same things a rest stops for: a creature
  coming close, a blow, bad news. You cannot smoke in deep water.

A whole hour's smoke empties your belly to 1800 nutrition
points: Not hungry, 360 points above Hungry. That is about six hours of a human's hunger, and less
of a halfling's.

As your belly empties, your stats train. For every 100 points the pipe takes, you have a 25 %
chance of one [training mark](stats.md#raising-your-stats) in one of your six stats, picked at
random. From Bloated a full smoke takes about 3600 points; from a halfling's fullest belly (13000) about 11100. That is about nine marks at the least and about 28 at the most.

A smoke cut short counts for its share of the hour: stopped after 15 minutes, it takes a quarter
of what a full one would, and gives marks for that much.

## Making one

A halfling trained in [Woodcraft](skills.md#skill-woodcraft) can carve a pipe. No other race sees
the recipe. You need a knife in your pack, one billet of wood for the bowl, and 10 minutes. It is
an easy piece of work (see [How well it comes out](crafting.md#how-well-it-comes-out)).

You may trim the mouthpiece with a shard of bone, horn, ivory, or dragonbone, or a lump of amber.
Or leave it plain: then it is of the bowl's wood.

Better wood and a trim make a finer pipe. Each adds grades to how well the work came out, up to a
Masterwork. A failed piece of work still fails. The table lists every wood and trim that adds
grades; plain wood and an untrimmed mouthpiece add none.

## Enchanting it

A pipe can be [enchanted](enchanting.md#enchanting-an-item) as if it were armour. It takes
every [prefix and suffix](affixes.md#prefixes) that body armour, a helmet, a cloak, gloves, or
boots can take, except those that add to the armour's own Protection, which a pipe has none of.
Its magic works while it is ready in the Tool slot.

How many enchantments it can bear is its [capacity](magic-items.md#capacity): 2, plus 1 for a
Marvellous pipe or 2 for a Masterwork, plus 1 for an ironwood bowl. The best pipe bears 5.
