# Coins and gems

Coins and loose [gems](gems.md) are found by their own rule, apart from items. Every place that
gives loot also has a **purse**: so many coins and so many gems, each by a chance and dice of its
own, rolled after the place's items. A place's purse never takes from its items, and its items
never take from its purse.

## Talents that find more

Some talents find more gems: [Hoard sense](horoscope.md#talent-hoard-sense),
[Gem lore](talents-origins.md#talent-gem-lore), and
[Dragon's hoard](horoscope.md#talent-dragon-hoard). Such a talent makes gems come more often
wherever they come by chance, and where they come anyway, it makes more of them. It changes
nothing else: you find exactly the same items, and the same coins, with it as without it.

How the numbers work: a talent that finds 100 % more gems doubles the chance first. A floor that
gives a gem 10 times in 100 gives one 20 times in 100. Once the chance is certain, what is left
over raises the count. A kist, a [chest](chests.md#what-a-chest-holds) that always holds one to
three gems, holds twice as many: two, four, or six. Talents add up: all three gem talents together
find 250 % more.

Talents that find more items, or better ones, such as
[Treasure hunter](talents-mind.md#talent-treasure-hunter), never change your coins or gems.

## Every place's purse

Each row is a place and what its purse holds. "4 times in 100" means the coins or gems come that
often, and otherwise none. Dice are rolled as written: "1d3" is one to three. "Talents count"
says whether your own talents change what the place gives.
