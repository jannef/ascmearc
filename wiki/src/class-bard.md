## How to play

You can do a little of everything, and nothing best. You start with a blade, a lute, the spell
[Mend](#talent-mend), and four
[skill rolls](skills.md#improving-a-skill) a level. Your hit points are few, so pick your fights.
[Travelled hands](#talent-travelled-hands) at level 6 makes you a beginner with every weapon, and
your [songs](abilities.md#songs) come at 18 and 40.

## Abilities

The bard's [inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire) spends
[inspiration](inspiration.md).

The bard knows a little of everything and masters nothing. They are a wandering singer who has
handled every weapon once and picked up every trade a little.

The bard has two **[songs](abilities.md#songs)**:

- **[Inspire](abilities.md#ability-inspire)** (level 18) lifts you. It is paid in
  [inspiration](inspiration.md).
- The **[lullaby](abilities.md#ability-lullaby)** (level 40) makes [hostile](creatures.md#factions)
  hearers [drowsy](status-list.md#status-drowsy). It is paid in [mana](mana.md#spending-it).

[Songs](abilities.md#songs) tells them in full: what they cost, how far they are heard, how
[Music](skills.md#skill-music) lengthens them, and the odds of shaking the lullaby off. A
[virtuoso](#talent-virtuoso) (level 60) sings for nothing, a degree longer.

**[Travelled hands](#talent-travelled-hands)** (level 6) raises, once, every
[weapon skill](weapon-skills.md) and [fighting style](fighting-styles.md) the bard has at rank 0 to
rank 1. It never raises a hidden style. The bard is a point
surer with whatever comes to hand.

**[Jack of all trades](#talent-jack-of-all-trades)** (level 25) trains every
[skill](skills.md) the bard lacks at 1d4+1, except Sneak attack, the thief's. From then on they are
skills like any other: used, marked, and raised at a level.

**[Master of none](#talent-master-of-none)** (level 50) makes every skill the bard is
trained in count 5 higher.
